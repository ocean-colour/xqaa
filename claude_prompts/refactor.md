#### What the project is

XQAA reformulates the classical Lee et al. QAA. Instead of QAA's empirically-tuned,
step-by-step cascade (estimate `a(λ₀)` empirically → backscatter slope `Y`/`η` →
propagate `bbp` spectrally → regress `ζ`/`ξ` to split `aph`/`adg`), XQAA derives a
single algebraic relation from the QSSA (Hansen/Gordon) reflectance model
`rrs = G₁u + G₂u²`, `u = bb/(a+bb)`. Inverting the quadratic gives, at every
wavelength, `(aw + anw)/(bbw + bbnw) = D(λ)`. Because pure-water IOPs `aw`, `bbw` are
well known, two spectral limits then invert the IOPs directly:

- **Red (λ > 600 nm):** water absorption dominates → `bbnw ≈ aw/D − bbw`.
- **Blue (oligotrophic, Chl < 1):** water backscatter dominates → `anw ≈ D·(bbw + ⟨bbnw⟩) − aw`, with `⟨bbnw⟩` = mean bbnw over 600–650 nm.

The `G₁`, `G₂` coefficients are fit offline (per-wavelength `curve_fit`, then B-splines)
against Loisel+2023 (L23) Hydrolight simulations and shipped as `xqaa/data/qssa_*.npz`.

**Status of the writeup (`context/xqaa.tex`):** a July-2024 derivation sketch, not a
finished paper — title still "To be renamed"; `G₁/G₂` λ-dependence deferred; error
propagation only partly worked out (bbnw-vs-`G₁` sensitivity only); no validation /
results / figures beyond one QSSA-validity plot; Rrs→rrs coefficient written as `1.17`
vs. QAA's `1.7` (needs confirming — the code uses `1.17`).
